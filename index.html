Gestures
  • Tap shows or hides the buttons.
  • Ruler: drag to slide a reading line, then Copy.
  • Grid: drag to slide the grid onto an edge.
  • Angles: drag to move the vertex; twist two fingers to rotate.
  • Measure: drag across the object, then drag either end or the middle to fine-tune. Your finger can stay off to the side: the point moves with it.
  • Double-tap resets the current view.
